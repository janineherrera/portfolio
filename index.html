01 — About me

I care about the boring details that make interfaces feel effortless.

I’m a front-end developer and UI designer with six years of experience turning ambiguous product ideas into shipped, measurable software. Most of my work lives where design systems meet production code — tokens, components, accessibility and the unglamorous plumbing that keeps a codebase pleasant.

I care about performance budgets, keyboard-first interactions and interfaces that stay legible for everyone. I like small teams, fast feedback loops and projects where the constraint is a real one.

Outside the browser you’ll usually find me bouldering, developing film photos, or over-engineering my espresso setup.
