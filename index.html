01 — About me

About a year into building for the web — still early, and enjoying the process.

I’m a junior developer about a year into my career, working across both the front end and the back end. Most of what I know so far came from building small things all the way through and breaking them in interesting ways.

I started with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, then reached for TypeScript and React on the front end and Node and databases on the back. I’m still early in all of it, but I care about writing code the next person can read, and about the parts of the job that never show up in a screenshot — accessibility, performance, and not quietly breaking things.

Outside of work I’m usually working through courses, rebuilding the same small app in a different framework to see what actually changes, and reading more documentation than is strictly healthy. I’m looking for a junior full-stack role where I can keep learning from people who’ve been doing this longer.
